S-08 FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to the questions estimators ask most. A closer look at how Quantiify simplifies the work behind every estimate.

01Can I use Quantiify if my estimating process still requires manual takeoff?

Yes. Quantiify supports manual, AI-assisted, and hybrid takeoff workflows. Your estimator can review, edit, override, or complete quantities rather than being forced to accept an automated result.

02How does Quantiify handle projects with multiple sheets and document types?

Quantiify is designed to work with project document sets, not just isolated drawings. Your documents can be organized and processed together so the relevant sheets and information are available throughout the takeoff workflow.

03Can Quantiify support different estimating logic for different trades?

Yes. Quantiify supports trade-aware workflows where components, measurement methods, calculations, review steps, and structured outputs can be aligned with electrical, mechanical, architectural, and civil estimating requirements.

04Can multiple estimators or quantity surveyors collaborate on the same project?

Yes. Team plans support multiple users within the estimating workflow, allowing project work, quantity review, and approval processes to be managed across the team according to the selected plan.

05Which drawing and document formats does Quantiify support?

Quantiify supports project drawing sets in approved PDF and CAD formats.

06Can Quantiify generate BOQ-ready outputs for pricing and tender preparation?

Quantiify can turn reviewed quantities into structured BOQ outputs that can support pricing, reporting, and downstream tender or estimating workflows. The estimator or quantity surveyor remains responsible for reviewing and approving the final information.

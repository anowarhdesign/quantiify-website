S-02 Workflow

From drawing set to bill of quantities
in five commands.

  1. UPLOAD

    Upload

    Drop in PDF or CAD drawing sets. Multi-sheet, multi-document projects are fine.

  2. ANALYZE

    Analyze

    AI reads every sheet, finds symbols, components, runs and areas, and sorts them by trade.

  3. TAKEOFF

    Takeoff

    Counts, lengths and areas come back as quantities your team can review, edit and approve.

  4. ESTIMATE

    Estimate

    Quantities feed connected calculations and an integrated pricing workflow. No re-keying.

  5. EXPORT

    Export

    BOQ-ready output for pricing and tender preparation.
